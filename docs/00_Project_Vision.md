# Burnish

> **An Autonomous AI Governance Engineer for DataHub**

---

# Project Vision

## Overview

Modern organizations rely on data to make critical business decisions. As data platforms continue to grow, maintaining high-quality metadata becomes increasingly difficult. Missing dataset owners, incomplete documentation, outdated glossary terms, and broken lineage create governance gaps that reduce trust in data and slow down decision-making.

Burnish is an autonomous AI governance engineer designed to work alongside DataHub. Instead of requiring governance teams to manually discover and correct metadata issues, Burnish continuously scans the metadata ecosystem, identifies governance gaps, explains its reasoning, generates intelligent recommendations, and safely applies approved fixes.

Rather than replacing human expertise, Burnish reduces repetitive governance work so that data teams can focus on strategic decisions instead of manual maintenance.

# Problem Statement

Enterprise data ecosystems evolve rapidly.

New datasets are created every day.

Documentation becomes outdated.

Dataset ownership changes.

Business glossary terms become inconsistent.

Lineage information becomes incomplete.

As organizations grow, governance teams struggle to maintain metadata quality manually.

These governance gaps reduce trust in data, increase operational costs, delay analytics projects, and introduce compliance risks.

Although platforms like DataHub provide excellent visibility into enterprise metadata, organizations still need intelligent assistance to continuously identify governance issues and recommend practical solutions.

---

# Why This Problem Matters

Poor metadata quality affects every team that depends on enterprise data.

Analysts spend additional time understanding datasets.

Engineers struggle to identify responsible owners.

Business users lose confidence in reports.

Governance teams become overwhelmed by repetitive maintenance work.

As organizations scale, these problems grow exponentially.

Burnish addresses this challenge by acting as an intelligent governance assistant that continuously improves metadata quality before governance issues become business problems.

# Project Goals

The primary goals of Burnish are:

- Improve metadata completeness.
- Increase trust in enterprise data.
- Reduce manual governance effort.
- Provide explainable AI recommendations.
- Maintain complete auditability.
- Keep humans involved in governance decisions.
- Integrate naturally with DataHub.

---

# Core Principles

Burnish is built around six guiding principles.

### Human-Centered AI

AI assists governance teams instead of replacing them.

### Transparency

Every recommendation includes clear reasoning.

### Safety

No governance change should occur without appropriate approval.

### Trust

Users should always understand what Burnish is doing and why.

### Automation

Repetitive governance tasks should be automated wherever possible.

### Continuous Improvement

Metadata quality should improve continuously instead of relying on periodic manual reviews.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The MVP focuses on solving metadata governance problems inside DataHub.

In the future, Burnish aims to become a comprehensive AI governance platform capable of monitoring enterprise metadata continuously, collaborating with governance teams, learning organizational governance policies, and proactively improving data quality across multiple enterprise systems.

Our long-term vision is to make Burnish an intelligent governance companion for every modern data organization.

---

# Summary

Burnish is more than a metadata scanning tool.

It is an autonomous AI governance engineer designed to continuously improve enterprise metadata quality through intelligent analysis, explainable recommendations, and safe governance automation.

By combining artificial intelligence with DataHub's metadata platform, Burnish helps organizations build greater trust in their data while significantly reducing manual governance effort.
