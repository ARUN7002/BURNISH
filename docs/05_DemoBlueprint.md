# Demo Objective

The objective of this demonstration is to show how Burnish improves enterprise metadata governance by detecting governance gaps, generating AI-powered recommendations, allowing human approval, applying approved updates to DataHub, and recording every governance action.

The demonstration should be completed within three minutes while clearly communicating the value of Burnish.

---

# Target Audience

This demonstration is designed for:

- DataHub Hackathon Judges
- Enterprise Data Engineers
- Governance Teams
- AI Engineers
- Technical Reviewers

---

# Demo Story

The demonstration follows a real-world governance scenario.

A company has thousands of datasets inside DataHub.

Over time, metadata quality decreases.

Several datasets now have:

- Missing owners
- Missing descriptions
- Missing glossary terms

Instead of manually finding and fixing these issues, Burnish performs the work intelligently.

---

# Demo Flow

## Step 1 – Launch Burnish

Open the Burnish application.

Display the main dashboard.

Show that Burnish is connected to DataHub.

Expected Time: 20 seconds

---

## Step 2 – Scan Metadata

Click the **Scan Metadata** button.

Burnish connects to DataHub.

Datasets are analyzed.

Metadata is retrieved.

Expected Time: 20 seconds

---

## Step 3 – Detect Governance Issues

Burnish displays detected issues.

Example:

- Missing owner
- Missing description
- Missing glossary term

The dashboard summarizes:

- Datasets scanned
- Issues detected
- Severity

Expected Time: 30 seconds

---

## Step 4 – AI Recommendation Engine

Select one issue.

Burnish generates:

- Suggested fix
- Reasoning
- Expected impact
- Confidence level (if implemented)

This demonstrates explainable AI.

Expected Time: 35 seconds

---

## Step 5 – Human Approval

The user reviews the recommendation.

Click:

**Approve**

This emphasizes responsible AI rather than fully autonomous execution.

Expected Time: 20 seconds

---

## Step 6 – Update DataHub

Burnish sends the approved update.

DataHub metadata is refreshed.

A success notification is displayed.

Expected Time: 20 seconds

---

## Step 7 – Audit Log

Open the Audit History.

Display:

- Scan timestamp
- Detected issue
- AI recommendation
- User approval
- Metadata update

This demonstrates transparency and governance accountability.

Expected Time: 20 seconds

---

## Step 8 – Closing

Summarize the outcome.

Example:

- 50 datasets scanned
- 12 governance issues detected
- 12 AI recommendations generated
- 10 approved
- 10 metadata updates completed

Close with the Burnish tagline:

> **"Polishing Enterprise Metadata with Autonomous Intelligence."**

Expected Time: 15 seconds

# Demo Success Criteria

The demonstration is successful if the judges can clearly understand:

- The governance problem.
- How Burnish detects issues.
- Why the AI generated each recommendation.
- How users remain in control.
- How DataHub is updated.
- How Burnish maintains a complete audit trail.

---

# Demo Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Slow scan | Use a prepared demo dataset. |
| AI delay | Cache sample recommendations if needed. |
| Network issue | Run DataHub locally. |
| UI confusion | Keep the interface simple and focused. |
| Time limit | Practice the demo multiple times before submission. |

# Key Takeaways

- The demo tells one complete governance story.
- Every feature supports the three-minute presentation.
- Burnish demonstrates explainable AI rather than simple automation.
- Human approval and auditability build trust.
- The MVP focuses on quality over quantity.
